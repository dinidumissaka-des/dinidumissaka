# Page Specification: Home

## 1. Purpose
The primary landing page designed to establish credibility as a Senior UX/UI designer, highlight AI-workflow integration, and drive traffic to specific case studies.

## 2. Section Breakdown
### A. Hero Section
- **Content:**
  - Main Heading: "AI-Driven Design | Scalable Systems | Visual Storyteller"
  - Sub-heading: Short bio emphasizing 5+ years of experience and location (Dubai).
  - CTA: "Get to know me" (Anchor link to About section/page).
- **Visuals:** High-quality portrait or abstract UI graphic representing "systems."

### B. "What I Do" (Expertise)
- **Structure:** 3-column grid.
  - 1. User-Centric Design (Web/App/Design Systems).
  - 2. AI-Powered Systems (Automation/Scale).
  - 3. Visual Design (Branding/Storytelling).
- **Detail:** Each column includes an icon and a descriptive paragraph.

### C. AI-Driven Toolkit
- **Content:** Logos for Framer, Figma, Adobe, Webflow, Claude (VS Code).
- **Function:** Establishes technical proficiency in the latest design-to-code and AI tools.

### D. Projects (Design Highlights)
- **Layout:** Vertical stack or staggered 2-column grid.
- **Items:**
  - *Deriv* (Enterprise focus)
  - *Planr* (Claude AI built)
  - *EcoByte* (Sustainability focus)
  - *Taletellers* (Storytelling focus)
- **Requirement:** Each item must feature a high-fidelity mockup and a "Learn More" button.

### E. Social Proof & FAQ
- **Recommendations:** Carousel or list of quotes from founders/directors.
- **FAQ:** Accordion-style list answering common questions about tools, experience, and AI workflow.

## 3. Footer
